Choose the response behavior that fits your problem. F5 Distributed Cloud can return a direct reply without contacting the application, change routing or response data, or explain a security decision. Each guide helps you choose a behavior, configure the resource that produces it, and confirm the result.

Direct responses deliberately answer matching requests. Error mappings customize eligible failures generated by the edge. Transformations change a destination, metadata, or selected values; security controls decide whether traffic can continue.

**Qualified examples** demonstrate the measured status, body or browser behavior with a matching control and evidence of which component produced it. Qualification applies to those examples, not every setting on a page. CAPTCHA completion remains **un-verified**, DDoS is **un-verified** and deferred, and Bot Defense is **configuration-only**.
