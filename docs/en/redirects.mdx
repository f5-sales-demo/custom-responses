A route redirect sends visitors from `/old` to `/new` over HTTPS. The destination application supplies the page.

> **Visitor response:** `302` with a `Location` ending in `/new`.

The `redirect_route` selects `/old`. Its `route_redirect` chooses the protocol, path, and `302` status, which describes temporary movement. Check client behavior if preserving the request method matters, and ensure the destination does not redirect back to `/old`.
