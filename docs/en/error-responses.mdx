Customize eligible failures generated by the edge while preserving their status codes. Choose an error mapping when the edge has already produced an error; choose a [direct response](../maintenance/) when a route should deliberately answer a request without forwarding it.

The response producer and body supplier are separate: the edge produces an eligible status, and `more_option.custom_errors` supplies its body. Application-generated errors kept their application bodies in the measured examples.

## Choose a class or exact mapping

| Mapping key | Applies to | Precedence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `3`, `4`, `5` | Eligible responses in the `3xx`, `4xx`, `5xx` families | Fallback when no exact mapping exists. |
| `503` | Eligible `503` responses | Overrides class `5`. |
| `404` | Eligible `404` responses | Overrides class `4`; runtime example remains **un-verified**. |

A class `3` body mapping does not create a redirect or change `Location`. Each encoded body has a 65,536-character limit including its prefix; see [response-body encoding](../configuration-reference/#response-body-encoding).

> **Visitor message:** Exact 503 or Class 5 in the qualified examples.

## Eligibility limits

Origin-generated `302`, `404`, `418`, `500` and `503` retained their origin bodies. Parser-generated `400` and `431` retained generic edge bodies. Accepted mapping configuration alone does not establish that a response is eligible.

The path named `/fault/502` produced edge `503` with “Exact 503”; its name does not establish a `502`. A custom `502`, exact `404`, and the remaining family mappings remain **un-verified** examples. Detailed investigation belongs in [operator observations](https://github.com/f5-sales-demo/custom-responses/blob/main/operator/error-mapping-observations.md).

For deliberately generated `404`, `410` and `503` replies, the qualified [missing-resource](../missing-resources/) and [maintenance](../maintenance/) routes require matching body mappings. Those results do not imply origin-body replacement.
